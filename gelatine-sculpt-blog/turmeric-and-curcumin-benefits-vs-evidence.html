Turmeric and Curcumin: Benefits vs Evidence

What tradition and trials say.

Turmeric is a spice and traditional remedy, and curcumin is its best-known compound. See safety and interactions.

Absorption

Curcumin is poorly absorbed on its own, so many products add other compounds. Read the ingredients.

Evidence

Research on joints and inflammation is stronger than on weight. Read the ingredients.

Cautions

It may add to blood thinners and can aggravate gallstones. Read the ingredients.
