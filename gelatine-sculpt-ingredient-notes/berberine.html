Berberine

One of the four Gelatine Sculpt ingredients: a plant alkaloid studied for blood sugar.

About Berberine

In short: A plant alkaloid studied for blood sugar.*

Berberine is a yellow alkaloid found in several plants and has been studied for its effect on blood sugar. It is the most pharmacologically active name on the list and the one that deserves the most care.

See the ingredients and safety and interactions.

Why It Is Studied

Berberine has been studied for blood sugar, with modest and mixed results. See berberine and blood sugar: what to know.

Why It Needs Care

It can interact with many medicines. See berberine and medicine interactions.

Main Caution

Berberine can lower blood sugar further in people on diabetes medicine, can interact with many other drugs, upsets some stomachs and is not for pregnancy or nursing. Talk to your doctor and pharmacist before using it. See safety and interactions.
