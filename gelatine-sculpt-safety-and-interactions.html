Gelatine Sculpt Safety and Interactions: Who Should Ask First

A frank look at who may consider it and who should talk to a doctor.

Who May Consider It

Healthy adults aged 18 and over who have read the ingredients, are comfortable with an animal-derived ingredient and have checked with a doctor if they take medicine.*

Diabetes Medicine and Blood Sugar

Berberine and green tea may lower blood sugar further, which could push it too low. Ask your doctor and pharmacist first and never change treatment because of a supplement. See berberine and berberine and blood sugar: what to know.

Other Medicines

Berberine can interact with many drugs, and turmeric may add to blood thinners. See berberine and medicine interactions and turmeric.

Caffeine, Liver and Gallbladder

Green tea carries some caffeine and rare liver reports, and turmeric can aggravate gallstones, so ask first if any applies. See green tea extract.

Animal-Derived Ingredient

Gelatine comes from animal collagen. See gelatine.

Pregnancy, Nursing and Under 18

Berberine is not for pregnancy or nursing, and the product is not for anyone under 18. See the FAQ.

Reactions

Stop using it and ask a doctor if you notice stomach upset, a rash or any other reaction. See the FAQ.

Realistic Expectations

No weight result is guaranteed. See how to spot weight loss supplement hype, the before you order checklist and healthy weight daily habits.
